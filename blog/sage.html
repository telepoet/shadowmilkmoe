The Sage of Truth, a "Complex Cookie"

Crossposted from Tumblr, circa May 2026

Canon Sage of Truth is even more fascinating of a character than I ever previously envisioned.

Like, he takes the revelation that he's the singular instance of the Fount of Knowledge in all of time-space to not corrupt like a fucking champion, for one, even going as far as to exposit on his own mentality from the days he almost reached that exact same breaking point such that Timekeeper could better understand Ultimate Shadow Milk's own present methodology, and the contradictory nature of it therein. He just seems pretty nonplussed about the fact that he's apparently cosmically predisposed to being miserable and evil, you know?Timekeeper divulging that that even in universes where the Fount doesn't corrupt on his own terms something usually else spurs it on is what makes this especially existentially horrific! Delicious!

🙶No one tolerates my mistakes. So tedious and terrible...
Then why was I brought into this world?
Why I was created?
Why must I walk alone, carrying this crushing weight...?
Where are those who made me...?
Questions without answers eventually gave rise to malice.
The thought that it is easier to destroy what causes pain than to endure it. 🙷
- Sage of Truth

🙶 Is that what you think? 🙷
- Timekeeper Cookie

🙶 Oh, not anymore.
I found my own way to break free from that cycle. 🙷
- Sage of Truth (Wheel of Fate)

He's just so cheerily matter-of-fact about everything. The world, his past, and his present interests/intentions-but he doesn't tend to even be nudged into divulging any personal information to begin with, which is how we end up with this fascinating gray area on exactly what he's thinking and feeling at any given time and what his opinions on cookiekind even are. He's mild-mannered and accepting of his role as a truthsayer in an almost Buddhist fashion? But also, like, will readily turn around and concede that he would do the same amount of timeline editing that Ultshamil has if in his shoes. Total transparency in his interest in changing the past (to a "reasonable" extent).

🙶 The behavior is different. Of THAT one me...
He is trying to control the situation to an excessive degree.
Even though Cookies who can't keep up are being left behind. 🙷
- Sage of Truth

🙶 What would you do then? 🙷
- Timekeeper Cookie

🙶 Oh, I wouldn't employ such methods.
If I did, all the Cookies interesting enough to watch would disappear. 🙷
- Sage of Truth (Wheel of Fate)

I think this line in particular most clearly outlines the way Sage approaches the world; he's amicable, generally interested in the greater good, and all around pretty well-adjusted, but its clear that the way he survived his experience as the Fount involved him shifting into an inherently self-centered mentality. The contrast between what he himself states about how the Fount struggled because he had no personal connections, to the Sage finding it only Mildly Interesting when TK asked about him is really telling, to me? It strikes me as if he eliminated any amount of a shit he had to give about any other cookies from his mind provided they aren't able to keep in step with him intellectually, and any exceptions such as TK themself make for a fun novelty more than a long-term connection.

It's not clearly spelled out what precisely set him apart and allowed him to exist in this "miracle timeline", but I wouldn't be surprised if it centered around him reaching peace and understanding in himself, foremost; finding "enlightenment" by casting aside the burden of caring about what happens to other cookies as a result of the Knowledge he provides. I don't say this as a negative, mind you, but this particular egotistical stroke only strengthens the argument that he exists as an inversion of Pure Vanilla's Truth; his is a reflection of Reality without Compassion.
