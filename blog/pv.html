On Compassion, Love, and Pure Vanilla Cookie

Reblog to a bigger tumblr post about the pairing ShadowVanilla's current status in canon, circa August 2026

I think a lot of ship fans often fail to grasp that [Shadow Milk and Pure Vanilla] absolutely do not love each other in (current) canon - at least not in a way that would be conducive to a mutually fulfilling, healthy relationship. I've been thinking lately about that interview from some months back where one of the developers replied to a question about character relationships with "What kind of romance could the Beasts and Ancients even have right now?" (paraphrasing), and in the context of BY15 that evocation of "bad timing" already made enough sense, but what ToF 7 has now highlighted is that Pure Vanilla indeed does not understand Shadow Milk as much as he believed he did, and in turn he'd approached the idea of harboring a "friendship" with him on unstable and poor pretenses from the very start.

The one thing Pure Vanilla has correctly recognized - and in turn had a leg up on everyone else on while he was the Recluse - is that Shadow Milk is profoundly lonely, and much of his facade as the Beast of Deceit is rooted in both resignation towards his many "failures" and lashing out at others because of the crushing expectations he was once put under. Pure Vanilla recognizes these feelings in himself, too, and he's previously described Shadow Milk - especially his past identity - as being "alluring", something that he is invested into peeling back the layers on further. But now that he's gotten some actual insight into the struggles that the Fount experienced through the revelations had at the Academy, Pure Vanilla doubtless has a lot to reflect on as it concerns the basis of their relationship thus far.

One of the pieces of this that I really hope we get some more in-depth coverage of is precisely what failed about his previous attempts at outreach: In that simply offering to be the Beast's friend - to be a shoulder to lean on, someone who will listen to what he has to say - is not nearly enough commitment to the issue or comprehension of what Shadow Milk actually needs in order to actually start to heal, because what's worked for Pure Vanilla does NOT work for someone like Shadow Milk, foremost being: he needs someone who treats him like a normal, thinking, feeling cookie with his own wants, needs and interests, and for all of those things to be met with a sincere desire to learn and engage with them - beyond surface-level sympathy - as he sees fit to share.

The Fount of Knowledge was regarded by the masses as little more than a tool, and the Fount in turn felt his existence was defined only by his worth to his creators, who later seemingly abandoned him. Having company that would serve to humanize him (for lack of a better term) would do wonders for his state of mind, and Pure Vanilla is more than capable of fulfilling that, but it was a naive belief that just standing at his side would do the heavy lifting; it neglects of the dozens of emotional barricades and layers that comprise his current state of maliceStarting with THE LITERAL EMBODIMENT of his joy for Knowledge, which Shadow Milk severed from himself and left behind assumedly shortly before his corruption. This is what Pure Vanilla meant when saying he'd "never considered" "diving into the depths of someone's heart." - in the face of prolonged trauma, it takes much more than just saying you'd be there for someone to make a difference.

Beyond this, his priorities as of current are to understand Shadow Milk in full as it concerns defeating him, which obviously is not particularly conducive to a healthy relationship, either - though I can certainly see a through-line where knowing all of Shadow Milk includes his joy and capacity for good as much as it does the hurt and present attempts at destruction, given that we're already on track for a Beasts redemption arc.

Hopefully Pure Vanilla will learn to turn "let me be your friend" into "you have the choice to be my friend," and finally give Shadow Milk the option to decide something for himself (for quite literally the first time in his life).
